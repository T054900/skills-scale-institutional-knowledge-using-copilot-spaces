# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme's project management processes and guidance. The lifecycle provides a consistent way to validate work, plan delivery, track progress, release changes, and learn from outcomes.

## Process at a glance

1. **Initiate and validate** the work by clarifying the problem, desired outcomes, success measures, stakeholders, and initial risks. Agree whether it should move into planning.
2. **Plan** the scope and shippable increments, prioritize and estimate the backlog, define acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, and align on milestones, dependencies, and risks.
3. **Execute and track** delivery through the project board and small, reviewed increments. Use tests and CI to support quality, monitor progress, and raise blockers through the agreed escalation path.
4. **Release and verify** changes after acceptance criteria, CI, and security checks pass. Prepare release notes and rollback or mitigation plans, deploy, and verify the result.
5. **Reflect and improve** through retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents. Record learnings and track improvement actions with owners and success criteria.

Roles and responsibilities, communication routines, and risk and dependency management support the lifecycle throughout. Project Managers coordinate delivery and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates acceptance and quality; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams use regular delivery and stakeholder updates, a shared source of truth, and clear escalation paths to maintain alignment.
