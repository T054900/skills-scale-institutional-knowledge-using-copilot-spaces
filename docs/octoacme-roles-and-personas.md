# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. Role assignments may be combined or shared on smaller projects, but ownership and decision-making responsibilities should remain explicit.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers shape accessible, usable experiences that address customer needs and align with product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Use research and stakeholder input to understand user needs
- Create user flows, interaction designs, prototypes, and visual design specifications
- Consider accessibility and usability throughout design
- Validate design concepts and communicate design decisions and open questions

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to translate user needs and product outcomes into experience requirements and to validate design choices
- Collaborate with Developers to assess feasibility and clarify design specifications during implementation
- Work with QA/Testing to identify usability and accessibility scenarios for acceptance and testing
- Coordinate design reviews and dependencies with the Project Manager, and incorporate relevant feedback from Stakeholders

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical direction and help the delivery team make sound, maintainable design and integration decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design and document significant architecture decisions
- Identify integration points, technical dependencies, and engineering risks
- Support estimation and break down technically complex work
- Promote engineering standards, security, reliability, and maintainability

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Guide Developers through design and implementation while leaving day-to-day task ownership clear
- Advise the Product Manager on technical trade-offs that affect scope, outcomes, or sequencing
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on technical risks, dependencies, and their delivery impact
- Partner with QA/Testing on testability and technical quality and with Stakeholders when technical decisions require input or approval

---

## DevOps / Release Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps or Release Engineers enable reliable delivery and operation of software through deployment automation, environment readiness, and operational practices.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain or improve CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Prepare and validate environments and release mechanisms
- Support monitoring, observability, and rollback or mitigation planning
- Identify infrastructure and release risks and communicate readiness concerns

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers to make build and deployment workflows reliable and repeatable
- Work with QA/Testing to ensure appropriate test environments and release verification
- Coordinate release readiness, timing, and dependencies with the Project Manager
- Advise the Product Manager and Stakeholders about operational constraints or release impacts

---

## Data Analysts

### Role Summary
Data Analysts help teams define, interpret, and communicate evidence about product performance and project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurement and reporting needs
- Analyze product or operational data and explain findings and limitations
- Identify data quality gaps and recommend improvements to instrumentation
- Share relevant trends to support evidence-based decisions

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to define success measures and evaluate outcomes
- Coordinate with Developers on event instrumentation and data quality requirements
- Provide the Project Manager with relevant delivery or outcome signals for status reporting
- Explain findings to Stakeholders and help the team use evidence to prioritize follow-up work

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support or Operations Representatives bring customer-facing and operational readiness needs into project planning and delivery, and relay feedback after release.

### Responsibilities
- Identify support, service, and operational impacts of proposed changes
- Contribute to support readiness, internal guidance, and customer communications
- Surface recurring customer issues, incidents, and feedback
- Help assess post-release impact and identify follow-up needs

### How They Work with Existing Roles
- Share customer problems and support trends with the Product Manager to inform priorities and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate readiness tasks, ownership, and communications with the Project Manager
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to communicate reproducible issues and validate fixes
- Coordinate operational handoffs with DevOps / Release Engineers and keep Stakeholders informed of customer-facing impacts

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- A person may cover more than one role depending on project size; teams should still identify who owns each responsibility and decision.
